What is a Quibble?

Quibbles are the small and green, domesticated amimals
of the future.  Many live as household pets, while
others are treated as mere beasts of burden.  Still
others are trained to race for the betting enjoyment of
half the galaxy.  It is these quibbles, which comprise
a kind of futuristic horse-racing, that this game is
about.

--------------------------------------------------------

The Concept

In this game you control a professional gambler.  
Quibble racing is your life, and it is your goal to make
smarter bets, better decisions, and ultimately more cash
than your two opponents.  Fortunately, you have more
than a few tricks up your sleeve in order to accomplish
this...  

--------------------------------------------------------

How to Play

A	Setting up the Game

First of all, for all you mentally challenged people out 
there, hit 'Start' to begin the game.  But before you do
so, feel free to admire the amazingly animated quibble
running across the top of the screen.

Next, you can setup the players.  There will always be 
three characters in the game, each of whom can be either
human or computer controlled.  If you select 'Human,'
you will be asked to enter that player's name.  Easy
enough.

B	Taking Your Turn

Once the game has started, you will be brought to the 
menu screen.  At the top of the window, you will be 
informed as to whose turn it is.  Pictured on the left
will be the three quibbles participating in the next
race.  To bet on a quibble, simply click on it and
enter how much you want to bet in the relevant box.  

There are several buttons on the menu screen which I
will proceed to explain in detail:

i.	Loan Shark

If you find that you're strapped for cash during the
course of the game, you can borrow money from the Loan
Shark.  To borrow money, enter the amount you want in
the text box and click the "Borrow" button.  To pay back
the Loan Shark, enter the amount you want to pay back
and click "Pay Off."  Once  you've borrowed money, your
debt will go up accordingly, factoring in the current 
interest.

ii.	Special	

Clicking the "Special" button will give you a list of
actions you can take which will affect the outcome of
the race.  Choose the action you want (provided you can
afford it of course) and then select the quibble you
wish to use it on.  What exactly all these actions do
will be explained later on in this text file. If you
absolutely have to know now, go on and scroll ahead.

iii.	Information

Here you can purchase two kinds of information.
"Projected  Rankings" will give you a fairly accurate
prediction of the next race's outcome.  This is much
more reliable than basing your bets solely on win
percentages.  

"Betting Placement" will give you a report of how many
bets each racer has received and how much money they
total up to.  This is useful for determining what the
pay off will be if your quibble wins the race.

iv.	Sponsoring

There are two options here: "Sponsor a Quibble" and
"Current Status." The first option will show you three
quibbles available for sponsorship.  Once you've
sponsored a quibble it will show up under "Current
Status," where you can change it's name, or have it
trained.  You can only sponsor three quibbles at any one
time.

Any time a quibble you sponsored is involved in a race,
you will earn cash proportional to the quibble's win
percentage.  If your quibble actually wins a race, you
will receive a $100 cut of the prize money.

v.	Retire

If you're totally broke, and the Loan Shark won't loan
you any more money, you can quit the game early by 
hitting "Retire".  

When you're done with your turn hit "done" and the race
will begin!


--------------------------------------------------------

Betting Mechanics

All the money that people bet (that would be you, your
opponents, and 12 invisible gamblers) goes into a common
pool, which the winners divide.  How big a portion you 
will receive depends on how many other people placed
winning bets, and how big your bet was.  This means 
that quibbles which few people have chosen, while 
probably slow, will yield a much larger reward.

Special Descriptions

Although training your quibble is sanctioned by the
NQA (Nat'l Quibble Association), almost any other form
of tampering is strictly illegal.  However, if a gambler
is crafty enough, he can get away with anything,
provided he has the money...

1.  Steroids     $50     Illegal

A careful blend of chlorophetamines and wasp extracts
injected into the rump of a sleeping quibble can
drastically augment its stamina, if only for one race.

2.  Weights      $30     Illegal

These large, bulky, lead ankle weights can be attached
to the legs of a quibble to slow down performance.

3.  Poison       $150    Illegal

Although harmless to most humanoids, the milk of your
average goat is extremely deadly to the sensitive
skin of quibbles, killing them instantly upon touch.
In fact, even the smell of goat's milk can
sometimes force a relatively healthy quibble into
dementia.  Use with extreme caution.

4.  Train        $20     Legal

The NQA recommends a strict diet of Peanut M&M's to
give your quibble's pelt a healthy luster.  This,
accompanied by an aggressive training schedule and
an occasional beating to callous your quibble to the
harshness of racing, will have a positive effect on
its performance in future races.

5.   Cripple     $35     Illegal

Make a fist - that is the size of your average quibble's
kneecaps.  To break them, thugs use a special club made
from a tough titanium alloy. A quibble will never fully
recover from these attacks.

6.   Drug        $75     Illegal

One Wilma Flintstone vitamin, carefully dissolved in a
quibble's drinking water will render it to become
confused, and temporarily lose its sense of direction.
Drugged quibbles have a tendency to run backward during
a race.

7.   Guard       $25     Legal

While the NQA doesn't usually take an active stance
toward race tampering, getting an opponent caught in the
act by hiring someone to guard your quibble will result
in a substantial fine.

Additional Note on specials:

Specials do not occur the second you purchase them.
All specials take effect simultaneously before the
race begins.  This means that you can guard a quibble
from an action taken on a turn prior to yours.

Options

A.  Game Type

Play for Goal - The game ends when one player acquires
                specified amount of money.

Play for Rounds - The game ends after a certain number
		  of rounds.  The winner is the player
                  with the most money.

Start $ - The amount of money each player starts with.

Interest - The amount of money the loan shark will
           charge for each loan.

Speed - Specifies how fast the races will be.
	Range: 1-30

B.  Settings

Specials - Toggle Specials On/Off

Special Limit - Toggles whether or not you can use
                Specials more than once per round.

Information - Toggle Information On/Off

Sponsoring - Toggle Sponsoring On/Off
